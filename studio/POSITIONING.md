# POSITIONING — SHINOBI WAY: THE INFINITE TOWER

Product owns this file. Human signs changes. Growth may quote signed lines only.

**Status:** draft for first studio pass. Not signed.

## One sentence

SHINOBI WAY is a turn-based tower roguelike where you are a cutout shinobi against fog, capital, and void: each location rewrites you, each fight is a readable duel, each event is moral combat — and you always feel like you are *watching* a cinematic ninja-terror session, not filling a form.

## Pitch (public, pending Rights)

Fog, rust, and a CRT visor. Survive the tower as if you were always watching the match.

Do not ship this line until Rights marks it SHIP. It contains no third-party names; still needs human OK.

## Who it is for

A player who wants a tactical hand (approach, posture, jutsu) inside a painted seinen coast — not a shonen power fantasy and not a spreadsheet roguelike.

## What 20 minutes of Region 1 must feel like

1. A ninja cyber-terror game, not a prototype
2. Each site changed me a little
3. The techniques have weight
4. Tooltips teach without a wiki
5. The events mattered

If a stranger cannot say those five, there is no vision progress — even if new features shipped.

## Anti-vision

- Shonen karaoke / “be the anime hero”
- Empty-friction clicks
- Gore or cosmic dissolve with no material (rust and salt have form)
- SaaS glass UI / permanent spreadsheet HUD
- Abstract tower with no country
- New systems instead of making Region 1 readable

## Comparables (use these)

| Game | Why it is a comparable | What we are not copying |
|---|---|---|
| Slay the Spire | Readable tactical hand, every card a trade-off | Deck-builder identity |
| Buriedbornes | Abyss mood, visor-as-chassis | UI clone |
| FTL | Run as a thin plan that can collapse | Real-time crew micro |

Do not position against licensed anime games.

## This month vs theater

**This month:** make Region 1 honest in 20 minutes (fantasy, tooltips, events, combat readability).

**Not this month:** live-ops, battle pass, influencer machine, ads, a second region as the pitch.

## Bets (unsigned)

1. Region 1 is the manifesto. Later arcs scale that promise; they do not define it.
2. Combat is a duel: approach, posture, hand — not DPS theater.
3. Events are moral combat. Fights resolve danger; events resolve who you are on that coast.

## Non-goals

1. Do not add systems that add clicks without a decision.
2. Do not market with third-party IP names (see RIGHTS.md).
3. Do not let store copy sound like a generic “ascend the tower” roguelike.
