# RIGHTS — public vs internal names

Rights owns this file. Human signs every SHIP. Growth may only paste strings marked **SHIP**.

This is a ship/hold register, not legal advice. When unsure: **HOLD**.

**Status:** conservative draft. Nothing in the RENAME column is signed.

## Verdicts

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| SHIP | Exact public string, signed |
| RENAME | Internal stays in the build; public uses another string (proposed, still HOLD until signed) |
| HOLD | Do not use in store, social, press, trailers, or public screenshots |
