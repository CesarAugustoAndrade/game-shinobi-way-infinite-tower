## Context

Mode activation pays full HP. Gate Prep’s SOUL contract reduces the **next** Gate Mode **activation** HP cost by half (one-shot). Weight feed is T-016. This task adds the **pending HP activation discount** and applies it inside pure `activateMode` (or a thin wrapper) for Gate family Modes only.

## Spec

### Must exist when done

1. **Pending discount flag/bag (pure)**
   - e.g. `pendingGateActivationHpDiscount: boolean` or `{ remainingUses: 0 | 1 }` on a small pure type (combat-state field optional; pure bag enough for AC).
   - `armGatePrepDiscount(state) → state` sets pending true (from playing `gate_prep` or pure call).
   - Encounter reset clears it (document align with T-002 frontier when wired).

2. **Apply on Gate Mode activation**
   - When activating a Mode whose definition uses **HP activation** and belongs to **GATES** family (`gate_of_life`, `gate_of_limit` / `MODE_FAMILY.GATES`):
     - If pending discount armed: effective `activationCost.hp = floor(authoredHp / 2)` or `ceil` — **use `Math.floor` once and stick** (document in test; e.g. 15 → 7, 35 → 17).
     - AP and chakra activation components **unchanged**.
     - After a **successful** activation that consumed the discount, pending clears (one-shot).
   - If pending armed but activation **fails** (cannot afford even discounted cost, not-ready, same-family): discount **remains** (not wasted on failed attempt) — SOUL intent is next successful activación; AC must cover fail-preserve.
   - Non-Gate Modes (e.g. Shadow Clones CP activation): discount does **not** apply and is not consumed.

3. **Afford / pay use discounted cost**
   - `canAfford` and `payCost` both use the discounted HP so a player who cannot pay full 15 but can pay 7 succeeds when discount armed.

4. **Enqueue source**
   - Pure helper `onGatePrepPlayed() → arm discount` callable from resolve SUPPORT later; tests may call arm directly. Do not require live `useSkill` cutover.
