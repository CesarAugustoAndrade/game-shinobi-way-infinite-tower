   - `markEffects`: self mark id **`coated`**, duration **2**, consume on **IMPACT** of next Offensive (SIDE or ATTACK), payload **Poison 5×3** (stacks 5 duration 3 or mark `poison` family DOT). Prefer `targetActor: 'self'`.

   - Honest description: Coated 2; next Offensive on hit applies Poison 5×3 and consumes coat.

2. **`resolveSkill` SUPPORT**
   - Pay costs; `damageDealt === 0`.
   - Plant `coated` duration 2 on player.
   - No immediate enemy poison.

3. **Next Offensive impact package**
   - When resolving `cardRole === ATTACK` or `SIDE_ATTACK` with **≥1 hit** and player has `coated`:
     - Plant enemy **`poison`** (or DoT mark) duration **3** stacks **5** (Poison 5×3).
     - **Consume** `coated` at impact.
   - Full miss: **no** poison plant, **no** consume (impact rule — coat waits for a hit).
   - SUPPORT / MODE plays do not consume Coated for this package.
   - Without `coated`, baseline unchanged.
