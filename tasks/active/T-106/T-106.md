## Spec

### Must exist when done

1. **Authoring (`poison_fog`)**
   - `cardRole: CardRole.SIDE_ATTACK`.
   - Costs: **AP 2**, **CP 5**, **CD 3**, HP 0.
   - `baseDamage: 9`; single hit.
   - `allowedRanges: [CombatRange.MEDIUM, CombatRange.LONG]`.
   - On ≥1 hit: plant enemy **`poison`** stacks **6**, base duration **3**, family DOT. If enemy has mark **`mist`** at resolve, duration **4** instead (+1 opportunity).
   - Prefer `markEffects` + pure duration adjust when `mist` present (document field e.g. `poisonDurationBonusIfEnemyMark: { markId: 'mist', bonus: 1 }` or inline pure check).
   - Tags: NINJUTSU / POISON / FIRE as available.
   - Remove primary TRUE poison 18×4 / “ignores 50% def” identity.
   - Honest description: 9 M/L; Poison 6×3; +1 Poison duration if Mist active.

2. **`resolveSkill` SIDE**
   - Pay AP2/CP5/CD.
   - Miss: no poison plant.
   - Hit, no mist: poison stacks 6 duration **3**.
   - Hit, mist present: poison stacks 6 duration **4**; mist itself **not** required to be consumed by this skill.
   - Damage baseline 9 under hit port; no Mode require; no Terrain mutation.

3. **Compose**
   - Reuse poison mark id and requireHit plant; only duration differs under mist.
   - Do not invent a second DoT type or TRUE poison pipeline.
