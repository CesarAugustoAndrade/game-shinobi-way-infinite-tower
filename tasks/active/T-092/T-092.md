## Context

Sand Shield / Mud Wall plant absorb without dealing chip. Water Clone should be the **offensive water SIDE** that deals **11** at CLOSE/MEDIUM and, on **≥1 hit**, grants the player **Shield 15** — never interacting with Shadow Clone Mode charges.

1. **Authoring (`water_clone`)**
   - `cardRole: CardRole.SIDE_ATTACK`.
   - Costs: **AP 2**, **CP 6**, **CD 4**, HP 0.
   - `baseDamage: 11`; `hitCount` default 1.
   - `allowedRanges: [CombatRange.CLOSE, CombatRange.MEDIUM]`.
   - On impact (≥1 hit): plant **self** SHIELD mark absorb **15** (family SHIELD; id e.g. `water_clone_shield` or shared shield id — match T-064/T-076 convention; stacks/absorb 15 until depleted).
   - Remove primary STR +40% buff identity for pure path.
   - **Must not** declare `modeInteraction` with Shadow Clones / must not restore Mode charges.
   - Tags: NINJUTSU / WATER / DEFENSE as available.
   - Honest description: 11 dmg C/M; on hit Shield 15; no Shadow Clone charges.

2. **`resolveSkill` SIDE**
   - Pay AP2/CP6/CD; damage baseline 11 under hit port.
   - Hits ≥1: self SHIELD 15 present on `marks` (player target); Mode board / clone charges **unchanged**.
   - Miss (0 hits): no shield plant; costs still committed if legal attempt.
   - No Terrain/Heat; no clone charge gain even if Shadow Clones Mode is ON.

3. **Compose**
   - Prefer `markEffects` with `targetActor: 'self'`, family SHIELD, stacks/absorb 15, applied on impact path (`applySkillMarkEffects` with hitsLanded) — same family as Sand Shield.
