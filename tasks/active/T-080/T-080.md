## Spec

### Must exist when done

1. **Authoring (`water_prison`)**
   - `cardRole: CardRole.SIDE_ATTACK`.
   - Costs: **AP 2**, **CP 6**, **CD 5**, HP 0.
   - `baseDamage: 9`; hitCount 1; WATER; MEDIUM `allowedRanges` (catalog MEDIUM only).
   - Tags: NINJUTSU / WATER / CONTROL as available.
   - Stun contract: **70%** chance **Stun 1** on enemy after impact (≥1 hit); duration **1** not 2.
   - Remove primary STUN duration-2 effects identity as sole packaging.
   - Honest description: 9 MEDIUM; 70% Stun 1; cannot voluntarily increase range while Stun persists.

2. **`resolveSkill` SIDE**
   - Pay costs; damage path base 9 under hit ports.
   - On **≥1 hit**: roll 70% Stun 1 (forced RNG ports in tests).
   - On **full miss**: no Stun; damage 0.
   - On successful Stun: arm **prison range-lock** (self mark/flag id e.g. `water_prison_lock` or tie lock to enemy stun source id) while enemy Stun from this package remains.

3. **Voluntary range-up lock (minimal)**
   - While lock active: pure helper or existing move-legality path rejects **player voluntary** band change that **increases** range (e.g. CLOSE→MEDIUM / MEDIUM→LONG manual move).
   - **Decreasing** range / forced `bandMove` / card movement: not blocked by this task unless already shared.
   - When Stun expires/cleansed: lock clears (document if lock is derived from stun presence vs separate duration mark).
