### Must exist when done

1. **Authoring (`earth_decapitation`)**
   - `cardRole: CardRole.SIDE_ATTACK`.
   - Costs: **AP 2**, **CP 5**, **CD 3**, HP 0.
   - `baseDamage: 13`; `hitCount` default 1.
   - `allowedRanges: [CombatRange.CLOSE, CombatRange.MEDIUM]`.
   - `bandMove: { kind: 'PULL', steps: 1, requireHit: true }` (catalog “PULL a CLOSE”: one band from MEDIUM lands CLOSE; from CLOSE no move / no reaction).
   - `impactStun: { chance: 0.5, duration: 1 }` (prefer this over mute effects STUN 0.6).
   - Tags: NINJUTSU / EARTH / CONTROL / MOVEMENT as available.
   - Honest description: 13 C/M; PULL to CLOSE on hit; 50% Stun 1.

2. **`resolveSkill` SIDE**
   - Pay AP2/CP5/CD; damage baseline 13 under hit port.
   - Hits ≥1: apply PULL 1 (range may stay CLOSE); voluntary move flag **unchanged**; if band actually changed, range reactions may fire (existing motor).
   - Hits ≥1: roll Stun 1 at **0.5** via `impactStun` path.
   - Miss: no PULL, no stun plant.
   - No Terrain/Heat.

3. **Compose**
   - Reuse T-071 PULL + T-055 impactStun; no new enums.
   - Do not invent multi-step “until CLOSE from LONG” — skill is illegal at LONG.
